Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 October 2026

This policy explains how SmartBridge Properties collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when visitors, property seekers, listers, agents, landlords, developers, and administrators use our marketplace and portals.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information such as name, email address, telephone number, company or agency details, profile image, and sign-in identifiers.
  • Property information, including addresses, descriptions, prices, charges, images, videos, documents, unit availability, and listing status.
  • Enquiries and viewing requests, including visitor contact details, messages, proposed dates, responses, and follow-up status.
  • Technical information needed for security and service operation, such as authentication records, browser information, and basic usage logs.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and secure accounts; review, verify, approve, publish, update, or remove listings; connect property seekers with listers; manage enquiries and viewing appointments; prevent fraud and duplicate submissions; provide support; maintain availability and unit counts; and comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal basis and consent

Depending on the activity, processing is necessary to provide the service requested, to pursue legitimate interests such as marketplace safety and fraud prevention, to comply with law, or because you gave consent. You may withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis, without affecting processing already completed lawfully.

4. Google sign-in and service providers

If a lister chooses Google sign-in, Google provides basic account information such as name, email address, and profile image. We do not receive the user's Google password. We use service providers including Supabase for authentication, database and storage services, and Vercel for website hosting. These providers process information only as required to deliver their services and under their own security and privacy terms.

5. When information is visible or shared

Approved property details and selected lister business information may be displayed publicly. Enquiries and viewing requests are shared with the relevant lister and may also be accessible to authorized SmartBridge administrators for support, safety, and lead management. We do not sell personal information.

6. Storage, retention, and international processing

Information is retained for as long as an account or listing is active, as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, maintain transaction and approval records, or meet legal obligations. Because our technology providers operate internationally, information may be processed outside Nigeria with appropriate contractual and security safeguards.

7. Security

We use access controls, authenticated portals, row-level database policies, approval workflows, and restricted storage rules. No internet service is completely risk-free, so users should protect passwords, sign out on shared devices, and promptly report suspicious activity.

8. Your data-protection rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you may request access to or correction of your personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, request deletion where appropriate, withdraw consent, or request information about how your data is used. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

9. Children

SmartBridge Properties is intended for adults seeking or listing property. We do not knowingly create lister accounts for children or intentionally collect children's personal information.

10. Contact and complaints

To make a privacy request or complaint, use the Contact SmartBridge or WhatsApp option on the marketplace and identify the request as a privacy matter. You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission where applicable.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the marketplace, legal requirements, or service providers change. The effective date at the top will show the latest published version.
